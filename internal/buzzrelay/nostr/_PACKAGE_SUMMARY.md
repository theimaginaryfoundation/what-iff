# Package: `internal/buzzrelay/nostr`

## Role

The slice of the Nostr protocol the Buzz relay needs, with no network code: keys, NIP-01 events, NIP-42 and NIP-98 authentication, NIP-OA owner attestations and NIP-19 npub encoding.

## Responsibilities

- **Keys (`keys.go`):** secp256k1 keypairs (`GenerateKeys`, `KeysFromHex`), x-only public keys, `NPub`, and `NormalizePubKey` (hex or npub, checked on the curve).
- **Events (`event.go`):** `Event`, `Tags` helpers, the canonical serialisation whose SHA-256 is the id, `Sign` and `Verify` (BIP-340 Schnorr).
- **Auth (`auth.go`):** `AuthEvent` (kind 22242), `HTTPAuthHeader` (kind 27235 with payload hash and a nonce), and `Attestation` (`ParseAttestation`, `Verify`, `ValidAt`; `Attest` for tests and rigs).
- **Bech32 (`bech32.go`):** BIP-173 encoding for npub.

## Key types and entry points

- `Keys`, `Event`, `Tags`, `Attestation`, `AuthEvent`, `HTTPAuthHeader`, `EncodeNPub`, `DecodeNPub`, `NormalizePubKey`.

## Dependencies

- **Inbound:** `internal/buzzrelay`, `internal/buzzplugin`, `internal/datastore` (npub for `models.BuzzAgent`), `cmd/buzz-rig`.
- **Outbound:** `github.com/btcsuite/btcd/btcec/v2` (and its `schnorr`).

## Non-obvious decisions

- **The canonical serialisation is written by hand**, matching serde_json (which Buzz verifies with): Go's encoding/json escapes `<`, `>`, `&` and U+2028/U+2029, which would change the id.
- **What Iff never holds a person's key**: `Attest` exists for tests and `cmd/buzz-rig`; the product only verifies attestations the owner pastes.

## Testing

- `nostr_test.go` checks key derivation against a keypair generated by Buzz's own `buzz-admin`, the NIP-19 vector, escaping, signing and tampering, NIP-98 payload hashes and nonces, and attestation parsing and conditions.
